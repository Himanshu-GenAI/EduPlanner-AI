Capabilities

Engineered for Peak Performance

8 powerful tools built on established neuroscience principles to maximize focus, retention, and exam performance — from the first session to the final exam.

AI Timetable Generator

Calculates dynamic study hours per day, prioritizes weak areas, and builds a comprehensive weekly calendar balancing all your subjects by difficulty and deadline.

Smart Reminders

Timely browser toast alerts right before sessions begin to minimize procrastination and establish a powerful, consistent study habit loop that sticks.

Goal Tracking

Set weekly milestone requirements and track target vs. actual task completion rates in real-time. Visual gauges keep your academic ambitions front and center.

Study Streak Tracking

Gamified daily streaks, milestone rewards, and activity heatmaps create a powerful consistency engine that transforms sporadic studying into an unbreakable routine.

Exam Preparation Planner

Automatically prioritizes subjects with approaching exams and spaces out review cycles using spaced repetition science to reinforce learning when retention is most vulnerable.

Performance Analytics

Visual analytics with subject breakdown percentages, Canvas-rendered donut charts, long-term streak heatmaps, and a personalized focus index score updated daily.

Personalized Recommendations

AI identifies your weakest subjects and study patterns, then suggests optimal session lengths, technique adjustments, and content focus areas to maximize your improvement rate.

Subject-wise Progress Reports

Detailed progress tracking per subject — hours logged, tasks completed, revision coverage percentage, and predicted exam readiness score updated in real-time.
